Help improve detection?

When you tap “Wrongly filtered?” or “Clickbait?” , we send:

  • the video’s ID, title and channel handle
  • which rules matched and your strictness
  • extension and patterns version

No account, no browsing history, no IP stored. Channels you block are never sent.

You can change this anytime in the extension popup.
