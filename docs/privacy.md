# Privacy Policy for AlgoCleanse

AlgoCleanse is designed with privacy as a foundational principle.

## Summary

- **No IP Addresses Stored:** Our servers never store client IP addresses.
- **Local Preferences:** Your channel blocks, allowlists, and configuration settings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
- **Opt-In Feedback:** If you explicitly choose to opt in, anonymous feedback may be sent to help improve detection patterns.
- **No User Tracking:** We do not track browsing history, personal accounts, or individual user identities.

## Data Collected via Feedback

When you opt in to feedback, the extension may submit:

- Filter action type (false positive or missed clickbait)
- Video ID and title
- Channel handle (if available)
- Matched rule identifiers and score
- Extension strictness setting
- Patterns revision and extension version
- Browser name (Chrome or Firefox)

This data is used solely to maintain and improve clickbait filtering accuracy.
